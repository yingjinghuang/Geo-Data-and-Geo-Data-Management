Week 2: Vector Data — Geometry and CRS

How do we represent places, paths, and areas, and why does CRS matter for spatial measurements?

Week Content

This week introduces vector geometry, how we work with it in Python, and how coordinate reference systems affect spatial analysis.

  • Points, lines, and polygons as different ways to represent geographic features
  • Creating and inspecting geometry with Shapely, pandas, and GeoPandas
  • Geographic and projected coordinates, CRS, and coordinate transformation
  • Measuring distances, route lengths, and areas using a suitable projected CRS

Materials

Lecture slides · PDF Lab notebook · IPYNB

Lab

In the guided notebook, we use a synthetic field-survey example near Vienna to create vector features, work with CRS, measure distances and areas, and make a map.

For the checkpoint, compare three candidate survey zones. Each must cover at least 18,000 m². Among those that qualify, choose the option with the shortest supplied route. Follow the detailed instructions in the notebook.
