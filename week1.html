Week 1: Geodata in the Wild

What counts as geodata in the real world, and what do we need to know before using it?

Week Content

This week introduces the course through real-world examples of geodata and a framework for understanding the different forms in which geographic information appears.

  • OpenStreetMap, satellite and street-level imagery, LiDAR point clouds, GPS trajectories, and city open data
  • Four layers of geodata: source, information type, representation, and storage & access
  • Why geodata needs context, and why a dataset is not the same as a file or an API
  • Introduction to Jupyter notebooks and the first geodata lab

Lab

We follow the same workflow across four examples: acquire → load → inspect → visualize. The examples are vector polygons, raster imagery, trajectory data, and 3D point clouds.

For the checkpoint, choose one data form, change one visualization parameter, rerun it, and briefly describe the change and its result. Follow the detailed instructions in the notebook.
